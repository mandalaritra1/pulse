    Tips for a fast, clean reading
    • Rest your fingertip lightly over both the lens and the flash. Pressing hard squeezes the blood out and flattens the signal.
    • Keep your hand and the phone still. Resting the phone on a table helps.
    • Cold fingers give weak signals: warm them up first.
    • A reading usually locks in 7–10 s. If it's taking longer, adjust your finger slightly.
    • Estimate only, not a medical device.
